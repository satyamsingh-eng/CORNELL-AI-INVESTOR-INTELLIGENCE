# Cornell AI Investor Intelligence — C3A Labs

Public report: Cornell-connected AI investors at pre-seed and seed, US and international. Verified August 2026.

## What this is
A decision-first intelligence terminal built for C3A Labs (SARVAX — B2B AI agents for wealth management). It answers: which investors with a verified Cornell connection actively fund AI at our stage, who the decision-makers are, how strong our access is, and what to do next.

## Evidence standard
- Every claim: source + access date + confidence (CONFIRMED / LIKELY / POSSIBLE / NOT VERIFIED).
- Zero fabricated emails, LinkedIn URLs, degrees, deals, or check sizes. Unavailable = NOT FOUND / NOT VERIFIED.
- Contact data: Apollo-verified or publicly posted only. Apollo proves identity/contact, never thesis or affiliation.
- Five non-Cornell assumptions were caught and corrected by primary-source checks (published in the report's correction register).

## Structure
Executive command center → TOP 10 people → stage-gated firm board → Cornell network layer → contact register → 5-layer relationship map → filterable TOP 25 profiles → evidence → research gaps → next 10 actions.

## Notes
- Scope: US/international pre-seed/seed AI investors with Cornell connections. No cloud-credit content, no accelerators, no India-only prioritization.
- Methodology is replicable for Princeton / IIT (swap-token table in the source system).
- Self-contained single file: works offline, no external dependencies.
