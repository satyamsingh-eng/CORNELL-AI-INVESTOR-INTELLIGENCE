What Qualifies — the Audit Standard

Every claim in this report is auditable against this section, and the correction register shows the eight errors the process caught itself making.
Definitions (this report is auditable against these)
  • Investor: holds a current VC investment role (GP / MP / Partner / Principal) at an active fund. Alumni in finance, operators who angel, and university staff are classified separately (INTRO-CONTACT / ANGEL-OPERATOR / NETWORK) and never ranked as capital targets.
  • AI investment: 2–5 named AI-relevant portfolio companies with dated announcement links (2023–26). A website saying "we invest in AI" is not evidence. A general SaaS company is not classified as an AI deal.
  • Pre-seed/seed: current fund vehicle at that stage, or named pre-seed/seed deals in the last 24 months. Multi-stage firms qualify only via a dedicated seed vehicle or demonstrated seed deals.
  • Cornell connection: verified degree (Cornell source, fund bio, or press) = A/B; formal institutional role = C; documented ecosystem relationship = D; portfolio-company founder tie = E (excluded); nothing = G. A portfolio company having a Cornell founder is NEVER a Cornell affiliation of the VC.
  • Decision authority: LEAD (writes/sets the check) · DECISION-MAKER (partner with vote) · INVESTMENT PROFESSIONAL (sources, GPs decide) · INFLUENCER (senior figure, not day-to-day deal lead) · NETWORK (connector value) · OPERATIONAL (ops title) · NOT VERIFIED.
  • Warm introductions: DIRECT = documented institutional/personal path (verified). POTENTIAL = plausible documented channel (alumni office, trustee office) not yet confirmed live. NONE FOUND = no documented path. Nothing is asserted as warm that lacks a documented basis.
  • Confidence: CONFIRMED (2+ independent primary sources) · LIKELY (one strong primary) · POSSIBLE (weak/secondary only) · NOT VERIFIED. Never silently upgraded.
Source hierarchy

Cornell official source / fund team bio > funding announcement > reputable press > LinkedIn (discovery only) > Apollo (contact enrichment only — proves identity/contact, never thesis, stage, or Cornell).
